## Data Flow Diagram Level 2

The Level 2 Data Flow Diagram provides a more detailed view of the internal processes of Study Buddy.

The major processes were broken down into smaller steps to understand how information is processed within the system.

The detailed flow includes:

- Receiving study material from the student.
- Processing the uploaded resources.
- Preparing reference knowledge for the Teacher Agent.
- Receiving the student's teaching input.
- Extracting important concepts and information.
- Updating the Child Agent's memory.
- Generating test questions.
- Processing the Child Agent's answers.
- Evaluating the results.
- Sending the final results to the dashboard.

This level of the DFD helped us understand the detailed movement of data throughout the learning process.

---

## Detailed Process Flow

The detailed flow begins when the student provides study material to the system.

The Teacher Agent processes this material and uses it as the reference knowledge.

During the teaching session, the student provides explanations to the Child Agent. The Memory & Learning Engine processes these explanations and updates the Child Agent's knowledge.

After the teaching process is completed, a test is generated and the Child Agent's answers are evaluated.

The final results are then provided to the student through the dashboard.
