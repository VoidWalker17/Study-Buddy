# Week 3 Journal — Study Buddy

## Data Flow Diagram Level 1

Week 3 focused on preparing the Level 1 Data Flow Diagram for Study Buddy by breaking the overall system into its major processes.

---

## Data Flow Diagram Level 1

The Level 1 Data Flow Diagram expands the high-level view created in the Level 0 DFD.

Instead of representing Study Buddy as a single process, the system was divided into its main functional processes.

The major processes identified include:

- User Authentication
- Study Material Upload
- Resource Processing
- Teaching Session
- Memory Update
- Test Generation
- Test Evaluation
- Dashboard and Progress Tracking

This helped us understand how information moves between the different components of the system.

---

## Data Flow Between Processes

The student provides study material to the system, which is processed by the Teacher Agent.

The student then teaches the Child Agent, and the information from the teaching session is processed by the Memory & Learning Engine.

The updated knowledge is then used during testing.

The Teacher Agent generates and evaluates the test, after which the results are displayed through the dashboard.

Breaking the system into these processes gave us a clearer understanding of how data moves internally.
