# Week 5 Journal — Study Buddy

## Dashboard Development

Week 5 focused on developing the Study Buddy dashboard to provide students with a simple way to view their learning results and track their progress.

---

## Dashboard Design

The dashboard was developed to present important information from the student's learning sessions in one place.

The main elements included:

- Overview of learning performance.
- Baseline and final test scores.
- Learning progress.
- Access to previous scores.
- A simple and organized layout.

The main aim was to make it easier for students to understand their performance without having to navigate through multiple screens.

---

## Dashboard Functionality

The dashboard was planned around the main Study Buddy workflow. After completing a learning and testing session, students can view their results and get a better understanding of their performance.

The layout was kept simple so that the important information could be viewed easily.

---

## Challenges

The main challenges were:

- Deciding which information should be displayed.
- Keeping the dashboard simple and readable.
- Organizing the results clearly.
- Matching the dashboard with the existing UI/UX design.
