# Week 5 Journal — Study Buddy

## Dashboard Integration & Results Presentation

Week 5 focused on bringing the dashboard and past scores together to provide a more complete view of the student's learning results.

---

## Results Presentation

The dashboard was planned to display the results generated after the Child Agent completes a test.

The main information considered included:

- Baseline test score.
- Final test score.
- Previous test results.
- Learning progress.
- Performance comparisons.

The aim was to make the results easy to understand and help students review the outcome of their teaching sessions.

---

## Dashboard and Past Scores Integration

The dashboard and past scores features were organized so that students could access their current results as well as their previous performance.

The dashboard provides an overview, while the past scores section allows students to look back at earlier test results.

This makes it easier to understand performance across multiple learning sessions and supports the overall objective of Study Buddy.

---

## Challenges

The main challenges were:

- Organizing current and previous results clearly.
- Maintaining a consistent interface across sections.
- Deciding how performance comparisons should be presented.
- Ensuring that the dashboard and score history follow the same design.
