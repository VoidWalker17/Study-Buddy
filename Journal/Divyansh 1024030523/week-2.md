## Gantt Chart

The main task this week was to prepare the **Gantt Chart** for the project.

The major development stages were divided into different time periods so that the team could keep track of the project progress.

The planned stages include:

- Research & Planning
- System Requirements & Architecture
- Resource Upload & Knowledge Preparation
- Teacher Model Development
- Student Model Development
- Memory & Learning Engine
- Test Generation
- Performance Evaluation
- Dashboard Development
- Testing and Deployment

## Planning

For example, requirements and architecture were planned before the development of the different AI components.
