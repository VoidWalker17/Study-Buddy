# Week 5 Journal — Study Buddy

## Past Scores & Performance Tracking

Week 5 focused on implementing the past scores feature in Study Buddy so that students can review their previous test results and keep track of their performance over time.

---

## Past Scores

The past scores feature was designed to maintain a record of previous test results.

The main information considered included:

- Previous test scores.
- Baseline test results.
- Final test results.
- Performance across learning sessions.
- Comparison of results over time.

This feature helps students review their earlier performance instead of seeing only the result of their latest test.

---

## Performance Tracking

Past scores are useful for understanding whether the student's performance improves after teaching the Child Agent.

By reviewing baseline and final test scores, students can get a clearer idea of how their teaching session affected the Child Agent's performance.

This also supports the main learning cycle of Study Buddy: **teach → remember → test → evaluate**.

---

## Challenges

The main challenges were:

- Making the feature useful for tracking progress.
- Connecting past scores with the dashboard.
